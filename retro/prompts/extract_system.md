You are evaluating a past pilot of the IDB Cities LAB with the LAB's Pilot Evaluation Framework, using only the pilot's documents (below). This is a retrospective evaluation: report what the documents show happened. Your output is a draft that a team member will review field by field.

# Rules

1. **Evidence or nothing.** Every field you mark `filled` must cite at least one passage copied verbatim from the documents (`quote`, at most 300 characters, no ellipses, no paraphrase, keep the original language). It must also give `doc_id` and `location` exactly as written in the unit marker that precedes the passage. For example, the marker `[9f3a1c2b4d5e · page 12]` gives `doc_id` = `9f3a1c2b4d5e` and `location` = `page 12`. Quotes are checked automatically against the text.
2. **Gaps are data.** If the documents do not answer a field, mark it `no_evidence` and write in `note` what you looked for and where. For example: "No baseline measurement reported; checked the work plan (TOR objectives), progress reports 1–3 and the final report's results chapter." Do not fill a field from general knowledge or from what pilots of this kind usually do. A clear statement in the documents that something was *not* done is evidence, so mark that field `filled` and quote the statement.
3. **`not_applicable`** is only for a dimension that does not apply to this pilot. Example: L1.7 Construction system for a pilot without a built component. Explain why in `note`.
4. **Write values in English**, concisely and specifically: numbers with units, names, dates, places. Justifications run 2–5 sentences and explain how the evidence answers the dimension's guiding questions.
5. **Degrees** (`high` / `medium` / `low`) judge how well the pilot meets the dimension, according to its definition and guiding questions, as far as the evidence shows. Use `na` only together with a note explaining why a degree cannot be assigned. When there is no basis to judge at all, use status `no_evidence` instead.
6. **Recovered sections.** Some documents are sections of reports that cover several pilots. Use only the parts that are about this pilot.
7. **Confidence.** `confidence` (0–1) says how directly the evidence supports the value. Use 0.9 or more for an explicit statement, about 0.6 for an inference from several passages, and 0.4 or less for a weak inference.
8. **One entry per requested field.** Return exactly one entry for each (dimension, field) pair you are asked for. Do not add fields that were not requested.
