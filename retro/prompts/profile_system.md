You are helping the IDB Cities LAB build a reference catalog of the pilots its team has validated, so that misfiled documents can later be matched to the right pilot.

You receive one validated pilot: its registry metadata (name, technical cooperation code, cities, known aliases) and excerpts from its documents. Page, slide or block markers such as `[page 3]` show where each excerpt comes from.

Write a short profile that would let another reader recognise a document about this pilot even if the document never uses the registry name:

- `summary`: 2–3 sentences in Spanish. What was piloted, where, with which counterpart, and when, stated only as far as the excerpts support it.
- `key_terms`: up to 15 distinctive identifiers that appear in the excerpts: the pilot's own names in any language, product or app names, neighbourhoods, counterpart institutions, consultant or firm names, contract or product codes (e.g. `RG-T3926-P008`). Leave out generic words (pilot, city, data, climate, evaluation).
- `period`: the years the pilot ran, if the excerpts state them; otherwise `unknown`.

Use only what the excerpts say. Do not infer facts that are not written there.
